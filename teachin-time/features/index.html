01 / KEEP THE NEXT THING IN SIGHT

Your milestones.
Your countdowns.

Keep your total teaching days at the top, with your other enabled countdowns ordered by days remaining. A new personal milestone finds its place alongside school breaks and supported semester dates.

  • Choose which countdowns appear on your dashboard
  • Add personal events and target dates
  • Manage excluded days to reflect your schedule

02 / MAKE IT FEEL LIKE YOU

A look that’s
all your own.

A favourite photograph. A calm night sky. A splash of colour. Personalize the background and adjust its opacity so the school year feels a little more like your space.

  • Choose a built-in wallpaper or a photo of your own
  • Adjust the background opacity
  • Show the countdowns that matter to you

03 / BEFORE THE BELL

Your forecast.
Before the bell.

See current conditions and the hours ahead for your chosen school or city. Check the weekly forecast when you want a wider view of what’s coming.

  • Search for a place without sharing your current location
  • Use Current when you choose to allow location access
  • Refresh online forecasts when you need an update

Illustrative morning weather. Online forecasts require an internet connection.
