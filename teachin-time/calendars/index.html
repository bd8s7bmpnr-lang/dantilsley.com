A LITTLE CLARITY

About those numbers.

Teaching days are different from calendar days

A teaching-day countdown follows the selected school calendar and exclusions. It is not simply the number of dates between today and your target. Check the teaching-day end time in Settings if a count changes at a different time than you expect.

Break dates and return dates

Check the calendar source and the meaning of each displayed target. A break-related target may be the return-to-school date. Your count also reflects excluded dates, so compare the teaching-day count with your instructional schedule.

Semester One is school-specific

This countdown is available only where the selected calendar supports it. For example, the 2026–27 Terry Fox Secondary calendar supports a Semester One target of January 26, 2027.

School closures and calendar corrections

The bundled calendar cannot anticipate every closure or schedule change. School and district announcements are the authority. Update excluded days as needed, and send a calendar correction with a public source link to support.
