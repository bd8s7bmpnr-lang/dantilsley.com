Privacy

Plain-language privacy, app by app.

The main rule is simple: collect only what an app needs to work, explain it clearly, and keep each policy close to the product it describes.

General philosophy

These apps are focused tools, not advertising surfaces. They should avoid unnecessary tracking, avoid third-party advertising trackers, and describe any app-specific data use in practical terms.

  • Data use should be tied to a visible feature.
  • Privacy language should be understandable without legal translation.
  • Each app policy should stay aligned with its App Store privacy details.
